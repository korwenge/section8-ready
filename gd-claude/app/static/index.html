How it works

  1. Drag a box over part of your level.
  2. Say what you want, like "neon cyberpunk deco, purple and cyan glow" or "pixel art of a red dragon".
  3. Claude builds it. Check the preview, then Accept or ask for changes.
  4. Accepted changes go into your save when you close GD (or press Apply now).
